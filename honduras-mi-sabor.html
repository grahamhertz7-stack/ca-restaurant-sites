About Mi Sabor

A hidden gem
in Compton.

Honduras Restaurant Mi Sabor serves authentic Honduran dishes in the heart of Compton. Known for generous portions, house-made baleadas, and warm, friendly service that transcends any language barrier.

The pollo con tajadas alone is worth the trip. Servings big enough for two adults, seasoned to perfection, with a side of crispy tajadas. Everything is made with care — you can taste it.

Honduran Specialties

Pollo con Tajadas

Baleada Sencilla

Honduras's most beloved street food. A thick handmade flour tortilla filled with refried beans, crema, and queso.
