About Don Chema

South Gate's best
pescaderia.

Don Chema Pescaderia is a South Gate institution serving the freshest Mexican seafood in the LA area. From the fish market up front to the full-service restaurant in back, everything is made with quality and care.

Whether you're picking up raw shrimp to cook at home or sitting down for mojarras and camarones a la diabla, the quality is undeniable. Prices are phenomenal for what you get.
