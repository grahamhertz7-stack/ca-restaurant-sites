About Naka's

A family-owned gem in Compton known for perfectly seasoned pork chop breakfasts, juicy burgers, and fresh homemade cooking. The owner is hands-on and passionate about every plate that goes out. Generous portions, great prices — this is the kind of place you keep coming back to.
