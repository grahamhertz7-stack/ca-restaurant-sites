About La Ceiba

Authentic Salvadoran.
Made with heart.

Restaurante y Pupuseria La Ceiba brings the best of El Salvador to Compton. From hand-made pupusas to Salvadoran tamales, every dish is made fresh with care and flavor.

Owner Ms. Mendoza runs a welcoming, professional spot where the food and service both shine. Great atmosphere — a very chill place with delicious food.
